❓ How to Use the Flash Builder

📝 Writing your section

  1. Find your section (assigned by the owner tag next to your name)
  2. Write your update in the text area — rough notes are fine
  3. Click "✎ Rewrite (Amazon Style)" to get a polished version
  4. Copy the rewrite into your text area if you like it, or edit manually

💾 Saving your work

Click "💾 Save" regularly to avoid losing your changes. Saving syncs your content to the cloud — all team members will see your updates in real time.

🟡 Updating the status flag

Next to the owner name, use the dropdown to signal your progress:

  • ⚪ Not Started — you haven't begun yet
  • 🟡 In Progress — you're working on it
  • ✅ Updated — your section is complete and ready for review

⚠️ Remember: Always click Save after making changes. Set your status to "✅ Updated" when done so the team knows your section is ready.
